# Black Mass Energies — Seed Diligence Explorer

Interactive single-page diligence site for Black Mass Energies (battery black mass trading and LFP refining R&D, Bengaluru), built from the company data room shared via Samaveda Capital plus public market sources.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies.

Sections: company overview, black mass basics, operations, technology, market and policy, competition, revenue and MIS, cash flow, customers and suppliers, transaction explorer, export pipeline, patent and IP, deck claims check, risks and questions, glossary, sources.

Company figures are management-provided and unaudited.
