02 · Black mass basics

How a dead battery becomes a tradeable commodity

Black mass is the black powder left after spent lithium-ion cells are discharged, shredded and sieved. It holds the cathode metals and the graphite, and recyclers buy it on its tested metal content. Click a stage to see what happens and where Black Mass Energies sits.

Why chemistry matters

NMC/NCA black mass contains nickel and cobalt, so it is worth more per tonne. LFP (lithium iron phosphate) black mass has neither, so lithium is the only valuable metal and the economics are thinner. The deck says LFP is the dominant EV chemistry in India and targets it for R&D.

How it is priced

Buyers pay a "payable": a percentage of the market value of the contained metals, set after an independent assay (Black Mass Energies uses Inspectorate Griffith India). A trader earns the gap between the payable it gets from a recycler and the one it pays a processor.

04 · Technology

Two refining routes that do not match each other

The deck sells an LFP formic-acid hydromet route at bench stage (78% lithium recovery). The only patent filed covers a carbothermic route that yields a nickel–cobalt alloy and a manganese product. LFP black mass contains neither nickel, cobalt nor manganese.

Process as described

  1. Feed: LFP black mass (lithium iron phosphate + graphite).
  2. Selective leach: two-step formic-acid leach that dissolves lithium and leaves iron phosphate behind.
  3. Purify: impurity removal and solvent extraction.
  4. Crystallise: lithium precipitated and refined to battery-grade Li₂CO₃.
  5. Control layer: AI-assisted tuning of pH, temperature, dosage and kinetics.

Claimed advantages

Fewer steps than conventional hydromet, less mixed-metal dissolution, lower acid and water use, less wastewater, lower lithium and graphite loss, and four clean product streams instead of one mixed leach liquor.

Who else does carbothermal

Bengaluru's Metastable Materials, seed-funded by Sequoia's Surge, Speciale Invest and Theia Ventures, has pitched a chemical-free "integrated carbothermal reduction" process since 2022–23. The same core idea is already being commercialised by a funded peer.

05 · Market and policy

Big tailwinds, but policy favours refiners over traders

India's rules push producers to recycle and push value-add onshore. That helps refiners. It also restricts black mass exports and excludes black-mass-only players from the ₹1,500 Cr recycling subsidy. Both hit the parts of Black Mass Energies' plan that exist today.

Why now, per the deck

  • FAME III, PLI for advanced chemistry cells and mandatory EPR force OEMs to deal with battery waste.
  • Critical-mineral security: cutting dependence on Chinese lithium carbonate.
  • EU Battery Regulation recycled-content thresholds create an export premium for certified material.

Where BME could defend a position

  • Aggregating fragmented black mass from small R2 processors and traders for recyclers that want steady feed.
  • Assay-backed quality and settlement speed: buyers pay, suppliers are paid within a day.
  • A future LFP niche, if bench results scale.

Where it is exposed

  • Refiners can buy directly from the same processors, and many already source from aggregators themselves.
  • No owned plant, licence or patent that a buyer needs.
  • Its customers are building capacity that competes with its refining plan.

It is a SaaS template

    What would make it investable

    15 · Glossary

    Terms used on this page

    Black mass
    Powder from shredded Li-ion cells: cathode metals plus graphite. Feedstock for refiners.
    Hydromet (hydrometallurgy)
    Recovering metals by leaching them into solution with acids or other chemicals, then purifying and precipitating them.
    Carbothermic reduction
    Heating with carbon (here, the battery's own graphite) to strip oxygen from metal oxides, turning them into metals or simpler compounds.
    LFP / NMC
    Lithium iron phosphate and nickel-manganese-cobalt cathode chemistries. NMC black mass is worth more per tonne.
    MHP
    Mixed hydroxide precipitate: a nickel–cobalt intermediate sold to refiners.
    Payable
    Share of contained metal value a buyer pays for black mass, set after assay.
    R2 / R4 recycler
    R2 dismantles and separates to black mass. R4 runs end to end, including refining to metals or salts.
    EPR
    Extended Producer Responsibility: battery producers must fund collection and recycling, proven with certificates from registered recyclers.
    CMRIS
    Critical Mineral Recycling Incentive Scheme, ₹1,500 Cr, capex and opex subsidies for refiners only.
    SMM
    Shanghai Metals Market, a price-reporting agency used as an index in contracts.
    FOB / T/T / B/L
    Free on board (seller delivers to the ship) / telegraphic bank transfer / Bill of Lading, the shipping title document.
    COA
    Certificate of Analysis: lab report on a product's composition and purity.
    FC-GPR / FIRC
    RBI filing after issuing shares to a foreign investor / bank certificate proving inward foreign remittance.
    MIS
    Management information system: the company's internal monthly report.
